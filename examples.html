How it works

How Command Room knows you.

Every Command Room install starts with one thing: a working folder on your computer that holds everything about your business. The AI reads from it on every interaction. That’s why nothing it produces feels generic.

  • People

    Your team, your clients, your investors. How each one works, what matters to them, the last six months of contact.

  • Projects

    Every active workstream. Current state, what got decided, what’s blocked, who owes what.

  • Decisions

    Every decision logged with the reasoning behind it. Searchable. So you never relitigate the same call twice.

  • Commitments

    A live ledger of what’s owed to you, what you owe. Captured from meetings and emails as they happen.

  • Voice

    How you write. Learned from your sent mail so drafts come back sounding like you, not like AI.

You use it two ways

01

What runs on its own.

Five scheduled chats fire on their own through the week. Morning brief, meetings, inbox, recap. Finished artifacts you read like any other message.

02

What you ask it for.

Ask anything, anytime — meeting prep, project status, follow-ups, a draft, a synthesis across people and threads. The answer comes back with your context baked in.

Once installed, your Chief of Staff runs five scheduled chats — four daily, one weekly. Each fires on its own, reads from your working folder, and posts a finished artifact you read like any other message.

02 · What you ask it for

The other half — ask anything.

The five scheduled chats run on a clock. The rest of the time, you talk to Command Room the way you’d talk to a Chief of Staff who’s been with you for years. Forward an email. Drop in a meeting transcript. Ask what you should do about a customer who’s gone quiet. The answer comes back with your full context loaded.

  • Generic AI A fresh chat. Knows nothing about you.
  • Command Room Knows you, your people, your last six months.
