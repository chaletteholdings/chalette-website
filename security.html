Chalette · Security & data

Your data stays yours.

Command Room handles the same things a trusted chief of staff would see: your email, your calendar, your deals, your numbers. So the first question is always: where does all that go?

Send this to your IT or security team →

The short answer: nowhere new. Command Room doesn’t add a Chalette server or a Chalette database to your life. It runs on Claude (from Anthropic) and reads and writes to a folder you already own. We never hold a copy of your data.

If you’re already comfortable using Claude with your own files, you’re comfortable using Command Room.

  • There’s no Chalette server.

    Command Room is a set of instructions. Plain-text files that tell the AI how to do your work. There’s no backend to break into, no account for us to hold, nothing running on a Chalette machine.

  • Your files live in your storage.

    Your Chief of Staff’s working folder sits in your own Google Drive, OneDrive, or local disk, under the same controls your company already uses. You can open it, read it, move it, or delete it any time.

  • We don’t get a copy.

    Chalette has no standing access to your inbox, your files, or your folder. We only ever see what you choose to show us in a working session.

  • Your data isn’t used to train AI.

    On a business plan, that’s contractual. On a personal plan, it’s a single setting we switch off together at setup. Either way, your conversations aren’t used to improve any AI model.

  • Leaving takes about five minutes.

    Revoke the connections, uninstall the plugin, keep your folder. There’s no Chalette data to delete, because we never had it.
