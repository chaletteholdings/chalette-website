Chalette · About

An executive AI implementation firm.

We help owner-operators and CEOs turn AI from a clever demo into an operating advantage: a system that knows their business, gets sharper the more it’s used, and runs real work instead of starting cold every conversation.

We work with C-suite teams, not just the person at the top. Through hands-on consulting and training, we get leadership leading with AI: making it part of how decisions get made, how work moves, and how the team runs day to day. All of it sits on top of your Chief of Staff’s working folder: your structured, owned context that every tool, every product, and every new hire can build from.

That foundation is what makes the rest durable. As AI advances, we build new products on top of the same folder, so the value compounds instead of resetting with each new model or platform. No matter where the technology goes, what we deliver is yours: owned, useful now, and worth more later.
