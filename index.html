Built so your data has one home: yours.

The memory lives in plain files on your own computer. Chalette runs no servers, holds no database, and never takes possession of your data. The architecture is the security model.

Your files, on your machine

Everything Command Room knows lives in a folder on your own computer, in plain files you can open, read, move, or delete at any time. There is no hidden store and no copy held elsewhere.

Chalette holds nothing

No Chalette servers, no Chalette database, no data passing through Chalette systems. Engagements run under mutual NDA, and the product is delivered into your environment, not hosted from ours.

One policy that matters

The core product runs natively in Claude on your own subscription. Your data is governed by Anthropic's enterprise policy: retained briefly, then deleted, and contractually never used to train models.

The honest version, because there are no magic bullets

Any hosted model means your prompts leave your environment to reach the provider. That is true of every AI product that is not a weak local model on your own servers. The question is who else is in the path, and here the answer is no one: your machine, Anthropic, done.

For organizations that cannot leave their own walls, including Microsoft-only and regulated environments, there is a path that runs inside your own tenant. It involves trade-offs, and they get named up front, not discovered later.

Where is the data stored
In plain files in a folder on the user's own machine. No external database, no Chalette-side storage.
Who processes it
Anthropic, under the same enterprise policy as your Claude subscription. Chalette is not a data processor.
Is it used to train models
No. Anthropic's enterprise terms contractually exclude customer data from training.
What does Chalette access
Working sessions are screen-share, under mutual NDA. Chalette holds no credentials, no API keys, and no copies.
What happens if we stop
You keep everything. The memory is yours, in your files, readable by any tool you choose next.
Microsoft-only environment
A tenant-side path exists for organizations that cannot move data outside Microsoft. Raise it early and it gets scoped honestly.

Built by an operator who needed it to exist.

Command Room was not designed in a product meeting. It was built by someone running real businesses who wanted an AI that actually knew his work, and could not buy one anywhere.

Matthew Davidov built Command Room for himself in January 2026. Running Chalette Holdings, a consulting firm with operating businesses and deal flow underneath it, he wanted what every operator wants: an AI that knew his projects, his people, his meetings, and his commitments, instead of a smart stranger that starts from zero every conversation. It did not exist. So he built it, on top of a file-based memory that lives on his own desktop, and used it every day to run his own work.

The business found him. He showed it to a business owner he was seeing deals with. A day later the question came back: are you selling this. The engagements that followed turned a personal tool into a practice. The first clients did not ask for more features. They asked him to do their executive teams next.

That experience set the model. What made those engagements work was not the software alone. It was an operator who has sat in the chair, who can talk with the accountant who has never touched AI and with the IT team in the same afternoon, pointing the tool at what each person actually deals with. That is why Chalette sells the product and the working sessions as one thing, and why the standard is value delivered, not licenses sold.

Chalette today delivers Command Room engagements under NDA inside some of the most demanding founder- and executive-led companies. The people who sit with clients are chosen for one ability: understanding the person across the table and the business they run. Builders support the product behind them. The conviction stays the same as it was in January: nobody knows which model wins, so the only things worth building on are your people's fluency and your business's memory.
