The five promises

What your Chief of Staff does for you.

  1. i. Meetings

    You walk into every meeting prepped. You never take notes again.

    Briefs drafted before. Recaps drafted after. Recall any meeting by person, topic, or date.

  2. ii. Projects & people

    Your projects and your people, organized and updating themselves.

    One live view of every workstream and key relationship. Always current.

  3. iii. Accountability

    Nothing slips. Every commitment tracked, every decision recorded.

    A live ledger of what’s owed. A searchable log of every decision and the reasoning behind it.

  4. iv. Inbox

    Your inbox is handled in your voice.

    Replies drafted in your voice. A queue you can trust to send straight from.

  5. v. Intelligence

    Your Chief of Staff gets smarter every week, surfacing what matters before you ask.

    The system learns your business. Soon it’s flagging things you didn’t know to look for.
